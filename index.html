How it works

Three steps. No upload required.

01

Point it at your folder

Drop Semester Shark on your laptop and choose your Documents/<uni> folder. It reads your slides, transcripts, briefs and outlines locally.

02

Wire your timetable

Paste the iCal link your uni already gives you. Lectures, tutes and exam dates appear in the calendar — no scraping, no passwords.

03

Let it plan your week

Set three sliders — target grade, current competence, time available. Study gets scheduled against your real assessment weighting.

Questions

The honest answers.

Does Semester Shark upload my coursework anywhere?

No. The AI runs locally on your laptop. Course materials, notes and study patterns never leave the device. There's no account, no signup, no sync.

Which Australian universities does it support?

Any AU uni where you can download your course materials and access your timetable via iCal — effectively all of them (UNSW, USYD, UMelb, Monash, UQ, UTS, RMIT, ANU, Adelaide, UWA, and others).

Do I need an OpenAI or Claude account?

No. Semester Shark ships with a local model. Optionally wire in your own ChatGPT or Claude account if you want — but it's never required, and your coursework still stays local.

What about copyright on lecture recordings?

Your downloads stay on your laptop. The agent reads them in place. We don't host, redistribute or store your university's content anywhere.

Why $9.99 lifetime instead of a subscription?

Because students hate subscriptions and we hate building churn-led products. Pay once, use it for your whole degree.
